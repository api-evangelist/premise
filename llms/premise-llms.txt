# Premise

> Premise is a global data-intelligence platform that combines machine learning with a distributed network of on-the-ground human contributors to collect, structure, and analyze local ground-truth data across 140+ countries. It serves precision mapping / geospatial intelligence, international development, and public-sector customers. As of this profile Premise publishes no public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDKs, or developer portal; data is delivered through managed engagements and its platform login.
